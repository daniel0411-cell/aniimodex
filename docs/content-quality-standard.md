# Content quality standard

Adopted 2026-10-23. Apply this standard to new pages and material updates.
It is an editorial and engineering standard, not a Google scoring formula.

## Before implementing

- Name the visitor, their question and the decision or task this page completes.
- Check whether an existing page already answers it. Improve or consolidate
  overlapping pages when they have the same purpose; preserve useful URL paths.
- Trace factual claims to a relevant source. Separate official records,
  community models, derived comparisons and unknowns.
- Identify added value beyond the source: a usable lookup, meaningful comparison,
  verified instructions, explicit curation or analysis that saves the visitor work.
- Match the title, metadata, links and controls to the actual available answer.

## While implementing

- Never fill missing data with plausible facts. Missing is not zero or confirmed
  absence. A disputed multiplier must not silently become neutral or enter a
  definitive ranking. Propagate uncertainty to dependent tools.
- Explain data scope and comparison selection. Do not turn shared labels into
  unsupported tier lists, recommended builds or guaranteed encounters.
- Attribution does not make copied descriptions original. Preserve source text
  where useful, but add a concrete way to compare, interpret or act on it.
- Do not pad prose, insert keyword variants or generate pages only to rank.
  Translation, automation and templates are not inherently disqualifying.
- Keep source-check dates separate from substantive page-update dates. An old
  snapshot remains old after a layout change. Do not imply live-game testing.
- Keep mobile reading, navigation and tool results usable. Empty-state text,
  deep links and section anchors must deliver what their labels promise.

## Before release

Validate representative complete, incomplete and edge-case records, all locales,
and mobile/desktop. Test shared calculations where errors change decisions.
Audit rendered HTML as well as interactions. Record unresolved evidence gaps;
do not hide them behind a site-wide approval badge.

Search indexing and AdSense review are separate outcomes. Neither a source-count
gate, mass noindex, more words, more articles nor traffic thresholds guarantee
approval. Do not submit another review just because code was deployed.
